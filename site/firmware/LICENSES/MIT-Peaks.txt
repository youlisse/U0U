The drum models of firmware/src/dbx_peaks.c (BD, SD, HH, FM) are ported from Mutable Instruments Peaks,
Emilie Gillet's eurorack code <https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack> (peaks/drums: bass_drum, snare_drum,
high_hat, fm_drum, excitation, svf; peaks/resources: lookup_tables.py, waveforms.py, which tools/gen_peaks.py
follows). Ported from C++ to C at 44.1 kHz for U0U, 2026. The ported file and the generated tables stay under the
